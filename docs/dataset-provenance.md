# Phase 4 dataset provenance

## What the 1,400 records are

The Phase 4 batch is fictional, deterministic test data generated by
[`src/reconx/synthetic/heldout.py`](../src/reconx/synthetic/heldout.py). It is not a
Razorpay export, a merchant statement, scraped data or customer data. The public
generator uses seed `20260901`, the `hold` identifier namespace and source indices
starting at `2000`.

This is intentional: the official AI Finance Controller track asks for a finance-ops
loop across a **50+ record batch of synthetic data**, together with match rate and
unresolved exceptions. It also asks for throughput, measured accuracy and an honest
exception list. See the [official Razorpay AI Buildathon page](https://razorpay.com/buildathon/).

## Exact record composition

The generator creates 110 settlement groups. Each of the 22 scenario families appears
exactly five times.

| Source collection | Records |
|---|---:|
| Orders | 330 |
| Payments | 345 |
| Refunds | 20 |
| Settlements | 110 |
| Settlement lines | 355 |
| Bank entries | 125 |
| Ledger entries | 115 |
| **Total** | **1,400** |

The count is not padded with copies of a single happy-path transaction. Extra rows are
created by deliberate scenarios such as duplicate delivery, conflicting duplicate,
multiple partial refunds, amount collision, duplicate bank credit, split ledger,
orphan ledger, corrupt input and equal-score ambiguity.

Raw ingestion accepts 1,335 canonical rows, observes 10 duplicate records, quarantines
60 raw rows and emits 95 validation findings. These figures are not additive partitions:
conflicting duplicates are counted as duplicate observations and their two conflicting
rows are quarantined. The finding count also includes follow-on warnings when a
quarantined parent leaves a settlement or entity reference unresolved. The full issue
ledger is retained in the Phase 4 machine-readable report.

## What “correct” means here

Every ID, timestamp and amount is fictional. The amounts are integer currency subunits,
and the expected credit for every group is derived from its settlement lines:

`captured payment gross - processed refunds - inclusive fees + adjustments`

For all 110 ground-truth groups, the expected amount recomputes from the raw settlement
lines and the labelled bank entry carries that amount. The candidate reconciliation
engine receives only the raw batch; ground truth is used afterwards by the evaluator to
score the prediction.

Not every observed row is supposed to be valid. That is the point of the evaluation:

- 65 groups are labelled safe for deterministic automatic closure;
- 45 groups contain seeded ambiguity, inconsistency or unsupported input and must not
  auto-close;
- the result reports all 45 as exceptions: 10 review-required, 30 unresolved and 5
  quarantined groups.

The fixed synthetic fee values exercise inclusive-fee and tax-component accounting.
They are test assumptions, not a quote for any merchant's Razorpay pricing. The field
semantics follow Razorpay documentation: amounts are in currency subunits, settlement
reconciliation exposes fee and tax, and payment `fee` includes its tax component. See
the [Settlement Recon API](https://razorpay.com/docs/api/settlements/fetch-recon/?preferred-country=IN),
[payment field reference](https://razorpay.com/docs/api/orders/fetch-payments/?preferred-country=IN)
and [settlement webhook reference](https://razorpay.com/docs/webhooks/settlements/?preferred-country=IN).

## Reproducibility and tamper evidence

The committed artifacts are:

- [`data/heldout/raw-batch.json`](../data/heldout/raw-batch.json)
- [`data/heldout/ground-truth.json`](../data/heldout/ground-truth.json)
- [`data/heldout/manifest.json`](../data/heldout/manifest.json)
- [`reports/phase4-heldout-evaluation.json`](../reports/phase4-heldout-evaluation.json)

The manifest records the generator version, seed, per-source counts, scenario counts,
and SHA-256 hashes of the raw batch, ground truth and frozen reconciliation policy. The
evaluator refuses to score if those values, batch identity, split label or schema
version do not agree. Tests also independently recompute every ground-truth settlement
amount and confirm the batch contains no customer identity fields.

## Honest limitations

“Held-out” means that the reconciliation policy was frozen before this separately
seeded split was evaluated. The generator and labels are public in the same repository,
so this is not externally sequestered, independently administered or production data.
The reported accuracy therefore proves reproducible performance on this declared
synthetic contract—not production accuracy on unknown merchant distributions.
